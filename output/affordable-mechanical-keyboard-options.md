## Fast Buying Guide: How to Choose the Right Board

To pick the perfect budget keyboard quickly, focus on these key factors:

### 1. Mechanical Switch Types
*   **Linear (Red):** Smooth, quiet, and fast. Best for gaming and shared office spaces.
*   **Tactile (Brown):** Includes a subtle bump when pressed. Ideal all-around choice for typing and gaming.
*   **Clicky (Blue):** Loud, satisfying click sound with a tactile bump. Great for feedback, but noisy.

### 2. Form Factor
*   **Full-Size (100%):** Includes the number pad. Best for heavy data entry and accounting.
*   **Tenkeyless (80% / TKL):** Drops the numpad to give your mouse more room to move.
*   **Compact (60%–75%):** Ultra-portable models that strip away non-essential keys to save maximum desk space.

### 3. Hot-Swappable PCB
Look for boards labeled "hot-swappable." This feature allows you to pull out broken or unwanted switches and snap new ones in without needing a soldering iron, greatly extending the lifespan of your keyboard.
