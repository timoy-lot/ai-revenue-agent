Programmer’s Buying Guide: What to Look For

When selecting an ergonomic keyboard, focus on these critical factors:

  • Split vs. Unibody Design: True split keyboards allow you to position each half shoulder-width apart to open up your chest. Curved unibody boards offer moderate ergonomics with a faster learning curve.
  • Programmability & Layers: Developers benefit heavily from keyboards that support layer switching. This lets you map bracket pairs, function keys, and navigation arrows right under your home-row fingers.
  • Switch Types: Tactile mechanical switches (like Cherry MX Brown) provide physical feedback without requiring heavy bottom-out force, preserving finger joints over long coding sessions.
