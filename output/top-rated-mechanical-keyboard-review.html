Quick Mechanical Keyboard Buying Guide

Selecting the right keyboard comes down to four core factors: switch type, layout size, build materials, and connectivity.

1. Mechanical Switch Types

  • Linear (Red Switches): Smooth, quiet, and consistent keypresses. Best for fast-paced gaming and silent office work.
  • Tactile (Brown Switches): Provides a subtle tactile bump on actuation. Ideal for general typing and balanced daily use.
  • Clicky (Blue Switches): Loud, satisfying click sound with a crisp bump. Loved by typists who enjoy audible feedback.

2. Form Factor & Size

  • Full-Size (100%): Includes a full numerical pad; best for data entry and accounting.
  • Tenkeyless (TKL / 80%): Removes the number pad to improve shoulder alignment and mouse space.
  • Compact (75% or 60%): Maximizes desk space while keeping vital keys tightly clustered together.

3. Keycap Quality

Always look for Double-Shot PBT keycaps rather than standard ABS plastic. PBT keycaps offer a subtle textured grip, resist finger oils, and prevent the letter legends from fading away over time.

4. Hot-Swappability

Always prioritize a hot-swappable keyboard. This feature lets you pull out old switches and pop in new ones in seconds, allowing you to experiment with different switch types without buying a completely new board.
