Quick Mechanical Keyboard Buying Guide

If you are looking to make a quick buying decision, focus on these three essential factors before choosing your board:

1. Switch Type (Feel and Sound)

  • Linear (Red Switches): Smooth, quiet, and consistent keypresses. Best for fast-paced competitive gaming.
  • Tactile (Brown Switches): Includes a subtle tactile bump on keypress. The best middle ground for daily typing and casual gaming.
  • Clicky (Blue Switches): Loud, snappy keypresses with clear auditory feedback. Great for typists who love loud mechanical sound profiles.

2. Form Factor / Layout

  • Full-Size (100%): Best if you frequently use a number pad for accounting or data entry.
  • Tenkeyless (TKL / 80%): Removes the number pad to give your mouse more room to move.
  • 75% / 60% Layouts: Compact designs built for minimalism and travel ease.

3. Connectivity

  • Wired: Delivers zero latency and eliminates battery maintenance—ideal for dedicated desktop setups.
  • Wireless (Bluetooth / 2.4GHz): Offers a clean, clutter-free desk and seamless switching between multiple devices.
